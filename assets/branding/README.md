# BiliComics Kindle Logo

[`bilicomics-logo.png`](bilicomics-logo.png) is the 1024 × 1024 project logo. It
shows 22 Niang and 33 Niang holding a single Kindle Scribe and reading manga
together. The two mascots form a frameless silhouette on a transparent canvas;
the Kindle is a small prop, not an enclosing frame.

The [native Kindle Library cover](../../packaging/kindle/bilicomics-library-cover.png)
composites this logo on white, converts it to 8-bit grayscale, and centers the
600 × 600 Lanczos resize on a 600 × 800 page. It is displayed on the native
Library card, not on the application's Shelf or sleep screen.

The PNG was generated on 2026-11-23 using OpenAI GPT-Image-2.5 Flare through
Magpie (`tx-jp/copilot/gpt-image-2.5-flare`), at `1024x1024` and `high` quality.
The original output is preserved, including its alpha channel. No reference
images were uploaded.
